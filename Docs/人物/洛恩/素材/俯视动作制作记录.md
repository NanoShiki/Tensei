## 行走提示词

Production game sprite sheet, EXACT 1024x1024 pixels RGBA transparent PNG. EXACT 4 rows 4 columns; every frame occupies 256x256 pixels. No border, grid lines or labels. Empty margins 16px inside each cell. Ground anchor at cell coordinate (128,224).
Use attached reference ONLY for red hair, ivory shirt, navy tunic, blue cloth sash, loose trousers, wraps and boots. REPROJECT to a genuine HIGH ANGLE TOP DOWN camera pitched 45 degrees down toward the floor, orthographic RPG 3/4 overhead perspective. Character never looks up at viewer. Large visible crown/top plane of head and shoulders, facial features on lower part of head, torso/legs strongly foreshortened by viewing from above. SIDE DIRECTIONS MUST ALSO SHOW TOP OF HEAD AND SHOULDERS FROM ABOVE. Compact four-head proportion. No horizon or ground plane. Nonpixel handdrawn anime cel art, clean flat color, no texture.
16-frame EMPTY HANDED WALK cycle. Row 0 walking screen DOWN south; row 1 screen RIGHT east; row 2 screen UP north; row 3 screen LEFT west. Columns: left foot contact, left-foot-support opposite leg passing, right foot contact, right-foot-support opposite passing. Feet alternate, arm swings opposite legs. Same character height and camera angle, no perspective scale drift. Entire figure centered consistently relative ground anchor, 16px safety padding. NO WEAPON, NO SHADOW, REAL transparent background alpha zero. No colored halo or white fringe. Generate final sheet at specified dimensions, not a character concept illustration.

## 规范化

原始输出 1254×1254；脚本定位主体，清理低可信 Alpha 和杂色边缘，32 帧使用同一缩放比例打包。主体包围盒底部及中心作为锚点估计，跑步第 2、4 帧离地 6px。脚本依赖 Python、Pillow、NumPy、SciPy，参数为两个候选路径。最终规格和检查见动作验证报告.json。
