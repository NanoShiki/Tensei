# 洛恩素材制作提示词

制作方式：内置生图工具。六张补充素材均以同目录已确认的 01-人物设计稿.png 为身份与服装参考。01、03 的原始提示词见人物设计稿制作记录与初始原画制作记录。提示词描述制作目标，实际符合程度以素材清单和检查记录为准。

## 02-表情设定

One clean character expression model sheet on uniform ivory background, exact 3 columns by 2 rows of same-size busts: calm, joyful, confused, focused determined, startled, bashful. All 6 same approved costume and youthful face. No lettering. Preserve approved reference identity, costume, youthful red-haired amber-eyed Lorn. Clean Japanese anime cel illustration, smooth solid fills, large coherent shadows, restrained detail. No grain, fragmented lighting, tie or leather belt.

## 04-成长原画

One cinematic landscape anime key illustration. Same young apprentice a little further in his sword training, same face and SAME APPROVED CLOTHING, steady determined protective stance with wooden sword ready held two-handed. Waist-up close shot, looking toward unseen challenge off left, no eye contact with viewer, serious focused eyes. Simple medium-detail fantasy sword-school arched entrance, wooden rack, grouped green canopy, stone steps. Represents mastery of basic training, no new class or costume. Rich designed composition and clean broad daylight shadow planes, restrained background detail. Preserve approved reference youthful identity and costume. Clean Japanese anime illustration, no grain or fragmented lighting.

## 05-服装与武器设计

One clean flat costume construction reference sheet on solid ivory. Show isolated front/back navy wrap tunic, ivory collarless short sleeve undershirt, loose navy calf trousers, blue cloth sash, dark wrist wraps, pale calf wraps, soft brown boots and wooden practice sword. Logical spaced layout, same designs exactly as reference. Flat laid out clothing pieces, schematic reference for future 2D layering. No body anatomy, no labels, no extra equipment. Preserve approved reference identity and costume exactly. Clean Japanese anime cel illustration, no grain or fragmented lighting.
